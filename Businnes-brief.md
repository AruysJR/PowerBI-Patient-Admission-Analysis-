# Resource Planning Analysis: Business Brief

## Background

The management of Well-Life Hospital is preparing its operational and resource allocation plan for 2025. To support informed decision-making, they require a comprehensive analysis of patient admission records from the previous four years. The objective is to identify key trends in patient demographics, disease prevalence, and admission patterns that can guide staffing, medication procurement, facility planning, and overall resource allocation for the coming year.

## Analysis Objectives

1. What is the total number of patients admitted during the four-year period?
2. How many patients were admitted each year?
3. What is the overall average age of admitted patients?
4. What is the distribution of patient admissions across different age groups?
5. What is the gender distribution of admitted patients?
6. How many unique diagnosis were recorded during the four-year period?
7. What is the distribution of patient admissions by diagnosis?
8. What is the distribution of patient admissions by diagnosis group, using the following classifications?
   - **Chronic Diseases:** Diabetes, Hypertension, Stroke, Asthma, Ulcer
   - **Infectious Diseases:** Malaria, Typhoid
9. How many patients are we expecting in the first quarter of 2025?
10. What specific recommendations can be implemented to improve resource planning in 2025?
